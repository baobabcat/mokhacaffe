The site is technically healthy: all eight audited pages returned successfully, and the repository’s 157 technical checks passed. OpenSEO found four informational title-length notices, no warnings, and no critical issues.

The main gap was not a technical defect. The site had no central page answering the broad problem its future readers have: how to make better coffee at home. That hub is now published in the source, linked from the homepage and journal, and ready for deployment.

Verdict

The foundation is clean, but the site is too small to earn meaningful search visibility yet. Search Console recorded 19 impressions and no clicks in the latest complete 90-day period. OpenSEO recorded 38 backlinks from 36 referring domains, but no measurable organic traffic or keyword footprint.

The one thing

Build the “Better Coffee at Home” topic around one start-here page

The new /better-coffee-at-home/ guide now gives readers and search engines a central destination. Each subsequent practical guide should link back to it, and the hub should link out to every relevant method, bean, pod, and gear guide.

Next content: publish focused pages on coffee-to-water ratio, grind size, choosing whole beans, and pod compatibility. These answer narrower questions while strengthening the central hub.

Where to focus first

Start with practical home-coffee improvement, but treat “how to make coffee” and “coffee brewing methods” as long-term hub themes rather than near-term ranking targets. Today’s OpenSEO research found these more specific, lower-difficulty opportunities:

  • best whole bean coffee: 3,600 monthly US searches, KD 0; build a bean-selection guide organized by taste and brewing method.
  • reusable coffee pods: 5,400 monthly US searches, KD 0; publish a compatibility and use guide for readers choosing refillable capsules.
  • best coffee pods for Keurig: 2,900 monthly US searches, KD 2; compare pod choices by roast, drink size, and taste.
  • dosage café filtre: 1,300 monthly searches in France, KD 0; use it for a measured filter-coffee recipe in the later French-language rollout.
  • cafetière italienne utilisation: 1,000 monthly searches in France, KD 0; localize and expand the existing moka-pot guide in that later rollout.

Search volumes and keyword difficulty (KD) are third-party estimates. Treat them as directional, not as forecasts.
