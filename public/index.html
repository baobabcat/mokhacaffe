One harbor, three centuries of coffee

For roughly three hundred years, nearly all the coffee the world drank left through one small Red Sea port. Al-Mokha was not where coffee was born, but it is where coffee became a commodity — and where it got the name that still sits on café menus today, usually next to chocolate.

The real story is stranger and better than the menu version.
