A starting point, not a rule

For filter coffee, 1:16 is a practical place to begin. If the strength is wrong but the flavor is pleasant, adjust the ratio on the next brew. Keep grind, time, and water temperature steady while you test.

Water weighs about one gram per millilitre for everyday brewing, so 320 g of water is roughly 320 ml.

Diagnose flavor separately. If the coffee tastes sour, hollow, bitter, or dry, changing the ratio may not fix it. Use the troubleshooting steps in our better coffee at home guide before changing several variables at once.
